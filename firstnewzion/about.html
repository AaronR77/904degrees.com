ABOUT FIRST NEW ZION

One Church • One Vision • One Voice

ONE CHURCH • ONE VISION • ONE VOICE

Faith. Family. Fellowship. Service.

First New Zion Missionary Baptist Church is committed to Christian fellowship, discipleship, service, and a community centered on the Word of God.

OUR MISSION

Mission Statement

Our Mission is to be the best Christian fellowship on this side of Heaven. With this in mind, we commit to be dedicated, consecrated and devoted to the principles of God’s Word.

To cultivate Christian love and unity through the teaching of God’s Word. To reach the lost at any cost. To present our minds, souls and bodies as a living sacrifice, holy, and acceptable unto God, which is our reasonable service.

To lead by example. To practice what we preach, teach, and to be living epistles to be read of men.

To encourage and develop every man, woman, boy, and girl to give God their best in time, talent, and tithe.

To help train and develop every member to grow from babes desiring milk to disciples eating meat. To be passionately focused on the mission and the vision of the church.

OUR VISION

Vision Statement

To provide an educational focus that enables the church discipleship to fulfill the Mission Statement of the church.

To provide opportunities for the people of God to grow in their understanding of God’s redeeming love as revealed in Jesus Christ, and to be in right relationship with God.

To provide opportunities for the people to respond in faith and love to Jesus Christ as Lord and Savior.

To provide an environment of community that will foster love, caring, and commitment to God and to each other.

To encourage people to discover their God-given gifts and provide opportunities to use those gifts for kingdom building.

To provide learning opportunities that bring about wholeness for God’s people and opportunities to grow in the Word and apply that knowledge to daily life.

To commit to building the best Christian facility on this side of Heaven, enabling us to train, educate, and minister to the needs of the total man. To provide a safe haven for all mankind.
